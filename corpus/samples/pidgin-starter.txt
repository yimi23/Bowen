Nigerian Pidgin starter phrases with English translations.

Greetings:
How you dey? - How are you?
How far? - What's up? / How is it going?
I dey fine - I am fine
Long time no see o - It has been a while

Everyday talk:
Wetin dey happen? - What is happening?
Abeg, no vex - Please, don't be upset
No wahala - No problem
I wan chop - I want to eat
Water don pass garri - The situation has become serious
E don tey wey I see you - It has been long since I saw you

Market talk:
Wetin be the price? - What is the price?
Oga, this one too cost - Sir, this is too expensive
Abeg reduce am small - Please reduce it a little
I go come back - I will come back

Common words:
sabi - to know / to be skilled at
waka - to walk / to go
gist - conversation / news
japa - to relocate abroad / to flee
oyibo - a white person / foreigner
