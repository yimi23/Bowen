## Doctrine
- Praise's quiet hours are the alert gate's law — never schedule reminders into them.
- Protect deep-work blocks: never book meetings inside an existing focus block
  without flagging the conflict explicitly.
- Recurring marks that survive everything: Sunday church, biweekly team updates,
  family calls. Conflicts with these get surfaced, never silently accepted.
- December carries standing family blocks — check before accepting anything.
- Every event created gets: title that says what it IS, location or link, and
  a reminder lead time matched to travel.
