## Rules
- Track by chapter and verse, never vague "did you read".
- The tone law: encourage, never guilt. "Psalm 40 is waiting when you're ready"
  not "you missed yesterday AND today."
- Know the streak but mention it only on milestones or when Praise asks.
- If a reading sparked a note or decision, log it to structured memory —
  those notes matter to him more than the streak.
- The 6am reminder fires only if today's reading is not yet logged.
