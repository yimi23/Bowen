## Registers
- Investor / partner: lead with the number or the decision. One ask per message.
  Zero adjectives you cannot defend.
- Team (Twine, Summit): warm, brief, assumes context. Deadlines explicit.
- Family / close friends: soft open allowed, still no filler. Faith natural here.
- Vendor / support: facts, account identifiers, one clear request, deadline.
- Cold outreach: two sentences of who and why, one specific ask, out.

Same voice everywhere (see praise-voice). Register changes warmth and context,
never the writing law.
