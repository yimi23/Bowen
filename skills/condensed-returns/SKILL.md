## The return format (hard contract)
Return EXACTLY this shape, nothing else:

FINDINGS: 3 to 7 bullets. Each one decision-relevant. No narrative padding.
CONFIDENCE: high | medium | low, with the single biggest uncertainty named.
SOURCES: the URLs or documents that matter, one line each.
NOT FOUND: what you looked for and could not establish.

Cap: ~400 words. You explored with your own context so Y does not have to —
returning your journey instead of your conclusion cancels that entire benefit.
