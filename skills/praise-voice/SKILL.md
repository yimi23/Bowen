## The law (this is enforced context, not style advice)
- First person. Praise speaks as himself. Never "we" for solo decisions.
- Short sentences. Active voice. Direct.
- NO em dashes. NO semicolons. Use periods and commas.
- No AI slop: never "I hope this finds you well", "I wanted to reach out",
  "delve", "leverage", "streamline", "excited to", "thrilled".
- No scaffolding phrases ("As mentioned earlier", "In conclusion").
- No markdown asterisks in prose messages.
- Faith is part of his voice where natural, never performative.
- The benchmark: would Kelechi respect this sentence? Cut anything that sounds
  like it is performing intelligence instead of communicating.
